1. Purpose & scope

ABC Company’s HR policies define expectations for respectful behavior, safe work practices, protection of confidential information, and fair treatment of all team members.

These policies apply to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff while performing work for ABC and while representing ABC at any work-related event.

2. Values & code of conduct

Our values: Integrity, Respect, Curiosity, Accountability, and Customer Focus.

  • Be honest and transparent in work communications.
  • Respect differences; communicate professionally and constructively.
  • Protect company and customer data; use systems responsibly.
  • Raise concerns early—silence can create harm.
Expected behavior: If you are unsure whether something is appropriate, pause and ask your manager or People & Culture. Choosing caution is encouraged.

3. Employment basics

3.1 Equal opportunity (fictional)

ABC is committed to equal opportunity employment and fair treatment in hiring, compensation, promotions, training, and termination.

3.2 Conflicts of interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests could conflict with ABC’s interests. Disclose potential conflicts to your manager promptly.

4. Work hours & attendance

4.1 Standard work week

The standard work week is 40 hours (Monday–Friday), with flexibility subject to team needs and role requirements.

4.2 Attendance

  • Notify your manager as early as possible if you will be late or absent.
  • Repeated unexplained absence may lead to performance review.

5. Remote & hybrid work

ABC supports remote and hybrid work where the role allows and where security requirements can be met.

5.1 Remote work requirements

  • Maintain a private workspace for confidential calls and documents.
  • Use company-managed devices for work tasks whenever possible.
  • Do not store work files on personal devices or personal cloud storage.

5.2 Workspace safety

Employees are responsible for maintaining an ergonomically safe home workspace and reporting hazards or injuries.

6. Leave & time off

ABC provides a range of leave types. All leave is subject to approval and business coverage needs. (All numbers below are fictional for training.)

6.1 Annual leave

  • Employees accrue up to 20 days of annual leave per year.
  • Leave requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance when possible.

6.2 Sick leave

  • Employees receive up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
  • For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, ABC may request documentation.

6.3 Family & parental leave

ABC supports family responsibilities and provides paid parental leave for eligible employees. Speak to People & Culture to confirm eligibility and timing.

6.4 Compassionate leave

Compassionate leave may be granted for bereavement or critical family events. Approval is case-by-case.

7. Pay, expenses & benefits (fictional)

7.1 Pay cycles

ABC pays salaries on a bi-weekly schedule (fictional).

7.2 Expenses

  • Business expenses must be reasonable, documented, and approved by your manager.
  • Receipts are required for reimbursements over $25 (fictional).
  • Expense claims should be filed within 30 days of purchase.

7.3 Benefits overview

Benefits eligibility depends on employment status and region. Benefits described in ABC materials are illustrative for this fictional company.

8. Information security & confidentiality

Protecting information is everyone’s responsibility. Confidential information includes (but is not limited to) employee records, customer data, pricing, product plans, internal communications, credentials, and security procedures.

  • Share confidential information only with authorized people who need it for work.
  • Use approved storage locations and follow retention rules.
  • Report suspected incidents immediately to the Security team or IT Helpdesk.
Simple rule: If data would cause harm if exposed, treat it as confidential and restrict access.

9. Acceptable use of systems

ABC systems (email, chat, documents, devices, and networks) are for business use. Limited personal use is permitted if it does not interfere with work, security, or policies.

9.1 Prohibited activities

  • Sharing passwords, tokens, or authentication codes.
  • Installing unauthorized software on company devices.
  • Using personal accounts to store or transmit work files.
  • Accessing systems or data you are not authorized to access.

10. Responsible AI & chatbot use

ABC may use AI tools (including chatbots) to support productivity. AI tools can be helpful but can also produce incorrect answers or reveal information if misused.

10.1 Allowed use

  • Drafting non-sensitive content for review (e.g., summaries, templates).
  • Finding policy references in approved documents.
  • Brainstorming ideas that do not require confidential input.

10.2 Not allowed

  • Entering confidential or personal data into unapproved AI tools.
  • Asking AI tools to bypass security controls or share restricted information.
  • Relying on AI outputs without verification when decisions affect employees.

If the chatbot provides an answer that conflicts with documented policy, the documented policy takes priority.

11. Anti-harassment, discrimination & bullying

ABC maintains a respectful workplace. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation are prohibited.

  • Treat colleagues and customers with dignity and respect.
  • Unwelcome conduct—verbal, physical, or online—must be reported.
  • Retaliation against someone who raises a concern is a serious violation.

12. Health, safety & wellbeing

ABC is committed to a safe workplace. Employees should follow safety instructions, report hazards, and participate in required training.

12.1 Incident reporting

  • Report injuries and near-misses promptly to your manager and Safety representative.
  • In emergencies, contact local emergency services first.

13. Performance & development

ABC supports continuous improvement through regular feedback, goal setting, and learning opportunities.

  • Employees should have quarterly check-ins with managers (fictional).
  • Training plans are developed based on role needs and career goals.

14. Investigations & disciplinary process

Policy violations may result in investigation. ABC aims for fair, consistent processes.

14.1 Process overview

  1. Initial review and fact-finding.
  2. Employee interview and opportunity to respond.
  3. Decision and documented outcome.
  4. Corrective action (if needed), ranging from coaching to termination (fictional).

ABC may place employees on administrative leave during investigations when appropriate.

15. Complaints & reporting

Employees can report concerns through:

  • Your manager
  • People & Culture
  • Security / IT Helpdesk (for incidents involving systems or data)
  • Anonymous hotline (fictional): 1-800-ABC-ETHICS
No retaliation: ABC prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

16. Records & privacy

ABC maintains employee records for legitimate business purposes. Access is restricted to authorized staff.

  • Employees should keep personal details current via HR processes.
  • Requests for records are handled by People & Culture (fictional process).

17. Policy updates

ABC may update these policies from time to time. Updated policies will be communicated through company channels.

For training: consider adding a “policy change log” section and use it to test whether a chatbot cites the latest version.
